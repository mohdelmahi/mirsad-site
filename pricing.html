Pricing questions

Is Mirsad free right now?

Yes. During launch, the Chrome extension and every free tool on this site cost nothing. The prices on this page start when paid plans open, and early users get 30% off their first year.

What counts as a tracked product?

Any product you add to Product Tracker or set an Alert on, by ASIN or link. Starter covers 5 at a time. You can remove one and add another whenever you like.

What is a database search?

Each time you run a Product Database search with your filters, that's one search. Starter includes 50 a month. Professional has no monthly limit.

What does fair use mean on Professional?

Professional is for a seller's own research. Automated scraping, reselling our data or sharing one account across a team isn't covered.

Which marketplaces are included?

Amazon UAE, Amazon Saudi Arabia and Amazon Egypt, plus Noon. Every plan covers all of them.
