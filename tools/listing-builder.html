Listing Builder — Mirsad Analytics
Tools · Listing builder Live

Check your draft against Amazon's actual field limits.

Write your title, bullets, backend keywords, and description here. Each field checks itself live against Amazon's real character and byte limits, and flags backend keywords that duplicate words already in your title or bullets, since Amazon has already indexed those, and repeating them just wastes backend space.

This checks structure, not content. It doesn't score your listing against competitors or write copy for you, that needs live listing data the extension reads, not something a static page can do. Limits shown are Amazon's general platform-wide Seller Central rules, the same across AE, SA, and EG, not independently hand-verified by marketplace the way the fee schedule is.
