FAQ

Questions sellers ask

How much does Mirsad cost?

The Chrome extension and the free tools cost nothing. Paid plans start at $19 a month (AED 70) billed yearly, and early users get 30% off their first year with code EARLY30. See pricing.

Which marketplaces does Mirsad support?

Amazon.ae (UAE), Amazon.sa (Saudi Arabia) and Amazon.eg (Egypt). The extension and fee calculators are built and checked against all three.

Are the sales numbers real?

No, and we say so on the product. Monthly units and revenue are estimates based on BSR and always shown as a range. Price, rating, reviews, BSR, category and fees are real data from the page.

Where do the fee numbers come from?

Each marketplace's own published Amazon fee schedule, checked separately for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Egypt. Always confirm final fees in your Seller Central account.

Does the extension send my data anywhere?

No. It runs in your browser, reads the Amazon pages you're already viewing, and doesn't need an account or your Amazon login.

How do I install it?

It’s live on the Chrome Web Store. Click Add to Chrome, then open any amazon.ae, amazon.sa or amazon.eg search page and click the Mirsad button.
