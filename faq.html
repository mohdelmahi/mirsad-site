Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question here? Contact us directly.

Is Mirsad free?

Yes, during launch. The Chrome extension and every free tool on this site cost nothing while we launch. Paid plans will come later, and people who join early will get a discount on them. Install it now, and leave your email on the home page so we can send you the early-user discount. You can see the planned prices on the pricing page.

Which marketplaces does Mirsad support?

Amazon.ae (UAE), Amazon.sa (Saudi Arabia), and Amazon.eg (Egypt) — the three marketplaces the Chrome extension and fee calculators are built and verified against.

Is the sales and revenue data on each listing real?

No, and we say so on the product itself. Monthly units and revenue are modeled from a listing's BSR, not pulled from a real sales panel — no Gulf marketplace has that calibration data yet. Price, rating, reviews, BSR, category, and fees are the real, verified fields.

Where do the fee numbers come from?

Each marketplace's own official published fee schedule (sell.amazon.ae/pricing, sell.amazon.sa/pricing, sell.amazon.eg/pricing), hand-checked row by row, not one table reused across all three.

Does the extension send my data anywhere?

No. It reads pages you're already browsing and enriches listings using your own logged-in session, in your browser. There's no separate server it reports back to.

Is Mirsad on the Chrome Web Store?

Yes. It’s live on the Chrome Web Store: install Mirsad Analytics for free.
